THE REFRAME

Prayer journaling isn't about having the perfect words.

It can simply be a way to slow the noise down long enough to notice what you're asking, what Scripture is reminding you, what you're surrendering, and where you've already seen God's faithfulness.

A SIMPLE RHYTHM

Read. Reflect. Pray. Remember.

You don't need a complicated routine. Start with four simple movements.

  1. 1

    Read

    Begin with Scripture. Slow down enough to notice what stands out.

  2. 2

    Reflect

    What are you carrying today? What is the passage inviting you to notice?

  3. 3

    Pray

    Respond honestly. Your prayer does not need to sound polished to be sincere.

  4. 4

    Remember

    Record what you do not want to forget—gratitude, provision, lessons, and prayers you are still carrying.

WHY 90 DAYS?

Not pressure.
Practice.

Ninety days gives you room to return to the practice again and again—not perfectly, but intentionally.

Return to Scripture consistently Create space to come back to biblical truth regularly.
